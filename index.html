About MedPlus Hospital

World-class healthcare that still feels human — that has been our promise since day one.

Our story

Built on care, growing with our community

MedPlus Hospital began in 2010 with a simple belief: world-class healthcare should feel human. What started as a small neighbourhood clinic has grown into a modern multi-speciality hospital serving thousands of families — yet we’ve kept the warmth of those early days.

Every doctor, nurse, and staff member is chosen not only for their skill, but for their kindness. Because great medicine is about more than machines — it’s about people.
